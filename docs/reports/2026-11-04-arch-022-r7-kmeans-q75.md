What did the Q75 changes remove from GPU k-means' main-thread time under a slider, and what remains for steps 6
and 7? The changes (commits 4218b4eaa, 6a0cdde10, c2a2c29c5, c9933533b): random seeds in O(K) instead of a shuffle of
all N indices, `reserve` in the point capture, the Accept written in place without copying the property set (other
properties keep their revisions, only the color channel is marked dirty), one palette entry per cluster, a same-type
identity path in the scalar publication, and the undo records take the published snapshot by move.

## Result
All 28 processes valid (exit 0, their own binaries, all controls passed, the final run applied on Vulkan); the null
arms' comparison is "not shown" under the frozen rule (no equivalence is claimed).

| | 100 000 points | 1 048 576 points |
|---|---|---|
| Accept's publication | 4.5–4.7 → **2.1 ms**, faster in every process | 52.7–60.4 → **30.5–31.4 ms**, faster in every process |
| one capture and start (process medians) | 1.38–1.40 → **1.02 ms**, faster in every process | 16.4–17.0 → **10.6–10.8 ms**, faster in every process |
| frozen R7 verdict, baseline | not met (U 9.9 ms) | not met (U 147 ms) |
| frozen R7 verdict, candidate | not met (U 26.4 ms) | not met (U 142 ms) |

- The four U maxima come from single frames whose time sits in the platform phase (`platform_begin`: the window's
  event poll, or a wait while the window is minimized; 7.9 to 130 ms) or the present (16 ms), outside every k-means
  path. Such frames occurred five times in this session's 12 run processes, in both commits, never in its null
  processes and never in the S3c-3 run's 14 processes. Where the time accumulated is recorded, the cause is not
  traced; the frozen verdicts stand.
- Exploratory, not the frozen rule: without those stall frames only the candidate's 100 000-point verdict changes
  (U 2.9 ms, under 4 ms); the baseline at 100 000 points (U 5.1 ms, the Accept; U − B 4.4 ms) and both commits at
  1 048 576 (the Accept: 61 and 32 ms) stay not met. With the two phases subtracted instead, the largest frame is
  2.7–2.8 ms per candidate process at 100 000 points (baseline 4.7–5.0 ms) and 31–32 ms at 1 048 576 (baseline
  53–61 ms).
- Descriptive: the continuous frames' median drops from 1.7 to 1.3 ms and from 17 to 11 ms; the first start from
  3.5–3.9 to 2.8–2.9 ms and from 34 to 23 ms; at 1 048 576 points the renderer's execute in hold frames from 29–32 to
  23–26 ms (not explained; predicted unchanged); a 1 048 576-point run process takes 8.5–8.8 s of wall time instead
  of 12.4–12.8 s.
