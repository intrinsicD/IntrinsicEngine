# ARCH-022 R7: GPU k-means before and after steps 6 and 7

Date: 2026-11-04. Task: `ARCH-022` (Q74, Q75, Q77, Q79, Q80). Status: measured under the full pre-flight. A
descriptive before/after comparison on one host, Release; it follows the
[Q75 before/after](2026-11-04-arch-022-r7-kmeans-q75.md).

What did steps 6 and 7 (with step 1) remove from GPU k-means' main-thread time under a slider? The changes: a request
reuses the previous request's point capture while the cloud keeps its revisions (block 6, 88da9648e); the cull bucket
table is written when the buckets are allocated instead of every frame through a write that waited for the graphics
queue to go idle, with explicit cross-frame barriers and the hazards that wait had hidden fixed (block 7a, 2581c7bb2);
Reduce and Update record batches of up to 64 pages with bitwise-identical results (block 7b, d5383d3cc).

All three primary metrics read "faster in every process" at both sizes. The Accept reads slower in every process
(+15 % and +3 %), which is not explained (see the audit). The null arms' idle frames are faster too (median 0.26 →
0.20 ms; predicted: they no longer write the cull table).

R7 (longest main-thread stall under 4 ms), frozen verdict: 100 000 points **met** (largest frame 3.0 ms, the cold
first start); 1 048 576 points **not met** (the Accept's frame, 32.6–32.7 ms). The candidate's only frames over 4 ms
at 1 048 576 points are the cold first start (21.3–21.5 ms, the first capture, which cannot be reused), the frame
before the Accept (16.6–16.8 ms, 16.3–16.4 ms of it transfer collection; the baseline's were 10.9–16.6 ms, so this
stall existed before) and the Accept's frame (32.6–32.7 ms, its publication 31.8–31.9 ms).

## Limits
One host and session, three processes per commit, fixed order; descriptive readings, no probability attached. The
harness records serially (the parallel-recording barrier is not exercised), does not render the cloud and does not
record whether a capture was reused (inferred from the code: the cloud never changes). Predictions missed low for the
start at 1 048 576 points, the hold frames and the continuous frames (better than predicted), and missed for the
Accept, the 100 000-point cold start (one process at 3.0 ms against at most 2.9) and the null frames (one baseline
null frame of 3.8 ms against under 1 ms).
