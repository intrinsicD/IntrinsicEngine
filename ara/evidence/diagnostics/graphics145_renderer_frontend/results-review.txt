## Findings

**1. Sample order string is wrong (correction, not a blocker).** The draft says "ABBAABBAAB samples." The manifest `sample_order` and the attempt table both give before, after, after, before, before, after, after, before, before, after — i.e. **BAABBAABBA**. The letters are inverted. The actual balance and interleaving are fine; only the prose label is wrong. Fix the string.

**2. No-op direction is unstated.** The draft says "No-op ranges overlap" and "no memory or no-op speedup is claimed," which is honest but omits that the after no-op median is nominally **5.6% slower** (31.900 → 33.671 ms), driven largely by the 36.899 ms outlier in 07-after-4. With overlapping ranges and a ~2 ms absolute delta this is noise, but a reader recomputing from `median_reduction_percent` will find a negative number the prose never acknowledges. Add one clause: no-op medians differ by ~2 ms in the unfavourable direction and the ranges overlap; no no-op claim either way.

## Scope check

The declared scope — local, warm-input, settled incremental rebuild of `ExtrinsicGraphics` including archive link, single host, five samples per arm, no statistical claim — matches the manifest `scope`/`validity`/`host_noise` text and the measured artifacts. Exclusions (clean build, whole engine, importer fanout, runtime, GPU, cross-host) are stated in all three of report, manifest and record. The diagnostic phase table is correctly fenced as unmatched single-run traces from a differently configured (Vulkan/GLFW) compile line, with an explicit instruction not to sum or combine with target walls; that is the right handling and I would not upgrade those rows. Clang 23 as the selected compiler is consistent throughout; the minimum-Clang question is correctly declared inapplicable.

Optional, not required: memory is the one metric with clean separation (before ~1.800 GB, after ~1.708 GB, non-overlapping, ~5%) that the draft deliberately declines to claim. Staying silent is defensible given it is max single-process RSS rather than aggregate; if you want the conservative version, state the observation and the reason it is not a memory claim.

## Limitations of this audit

Read-only and arithmetic-only. I recomputed medians, extrema, reduction percentages, ordering and counts from the supplied summary and the single canonical record; I did not open the archive, re-run anything, or inspect the other nine records, the source diff, the runner, or the manifest file on disk. Claims resting on artifacts not shown here — BMI byte equality, compile-command identity, package fingerprints, libstdc++14 sort delegation, log/archive completeness, and the retained review packets — are accepted as asserted, not independently confirmed.
