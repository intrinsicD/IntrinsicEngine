## Smoothing differential

PMP's three smoothing passes were replayed from its unsmoothed tensor values.
The manual damped update

```text
new_i = 0.5 * old_i + 0.5 * cotan_weighted_neighbor_average_i
```

matched PMP's result to approximately `2.5e-8` relative L2. Replacing the value
with the full neighbor average, as Intrinsic currently does, differed from the
damped update on armadillo by 21.65% (`kmin`) and 12.34% (`kmax`). Applying that
full-neighbor update to PMP's raw values left only 5.10% and 2.57% relative L2
against Intrinsic, respectively. No sparse or dense linear solve occurs in
either smoothing path.

## Remaining estimator differential

- Intrinsic projects the accumulated tensor to an estimated tangent basis and
  diagonalizes a 2x2 matrix. PMP diagonalizes the 3x3 symmetric tensor and
  discards the eigenvector whose eigenvalue has the smallest absolute value.
- Intrinsic computes boundary slots directly and includes boundary support.
  PMP computes interior slots first and interpolates curvature to boundary
  vertices afterward.
- On PMP `hemisphere.off`, the 120 boundary vertices differed by 18.20%
  relative L2 for `kmin`; PMP and Intrinsic boundary medians were about
  0.99725 and 0.82112. `kmax` remained within about 0.19%.
- On armadillo, the final fields had 1720 `kmin` and 278 `kmax` sign
  disagreements.

## Cross-model and literature follow-up — 2026-08-12

The retained diagnostic normalized a deterministic, size-stratified 96-model
sample from the 1,989 OBJ files under the local dataset root, then ran the same
position-only triangle payload through Intrinsic and the local PMP revision.
Eighty models (2,530,726 vertices) were directly comparable. The 62 models with
no rejected triangle had worst relative L2 errors of `4.55e-7` (`kmin`) and
`2.23e-7` (`kmax`). Across 2,520,863 vertices more than four adjacency rings
from a rejected triangle, worst errors were `3.48e-6` and `3.25e-6`.

Large full-field outliers localized to 129 degenerate and 376 severely
ill-conditioned triangles. PMP also produced a non-finite field on one model;
Intrinsic failed closed with finite zero sentinels and quality diagnostics.
Three clean controls retained parity from uniform scales `1e-6` through `1e3`
with at most `4.29e-8` relative L2, and at scale `1e6` within `2.58e-6`, where
the small loss is consistent with public float position quantization.
