A persistent-negative label requires all six values negative, max-min <=1% of |native S|, and each reconstructed sum greater in magnitude than its recorded 256*epsilon_long_double*sum(abs(eigencomponent terms)). - Per-eigencomponent signed terms, retained rank, smallest/top eigenvalues and sorted cutoff-mask changes are recorded. Two largest loadings of the smallest retained mode identify a pair to inspect; that pair is not claimed to characterize every mode or cluster.
